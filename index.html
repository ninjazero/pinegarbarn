Host your Wedding at the Barn at Pinegar Farms. Located under 10 minutes from I-80 in Loomis this impressive small farm boasts spacious outdoor areas, exotic pool party atmosphere, and a large game room located inside the barn for games and activities. Amenities:
  • Swimming Pool
  • Trampoline
  • Open Grassy Area (2.5 acres in total) Lots of yard games.
  • Grape Vines and Fruit Trees including Satsuma Mandarins
  • Game Room (full size Billiards Table and other games)
  • Barn Space (upstairs and down)
  • Free Parking

Moon Glow Band

Make your special occasion more exciting with live music! Moon Glow is a quartet of professionals who are multi-instrumentalists and will feature sax, clarinet, flute, guitar, piano, bass, or drums. We are all vocalists. Our extensive repertoire of songs can be tailored to any event: Parties, weddings, birthdays or any holiday.
